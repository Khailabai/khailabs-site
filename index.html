The habit we teach

Right Job. Right AI. Right Way.

Three questions. Ten seconds. Teachable to anyone on your team — even the person who still calls AI "the chat-bot thing."

  1. 01

    Right Job

    Should an AI even do this? Some tasks are too quick, too sensitive, or too human. AI is a tool, not a reflex.

  2. 02

    Right AI

    Think of AI tools as employees with different specialties. ChatGPT writes. Copilot works inside Microsoft. Gemini works inside Google. Perplexity researches. Wrong tool = confidently wrong output.

  3. 03

    Right Way

    Give it a role, give it context, give it constraints — then verify before it leaves your hands. Especially anything with a name, a number, or a dollar sign.

"AI will lie to you with confidence. Not because it's malicious — because it doesn't always know what it doesn't know." — Jorge, KhaiLab
